# If Insurance Challenge — Brief & Concept Framework

## My notes

> **Core Philosophy:**
> Insurance shouldn't only come to mind when buying a policy or filing a claim. Instead, it should be top-of-mind before meaningful life milestones or major decisions, actively empowering users with proactive risk intelligence and support—potentially shaping their decisions for the better.

**Examples:**

- **Moving:** Proactively identify coverage and logistical risks for the new home.
- **Buying a home:** Understand risks related to property, housing structures, and liabilities.
- **Getting a pet:** Anticipate medical costs, liabilities, and safety measures.
- **Starting a new job:** Re-evaluate personal liability, health, and income protection needs.

_(Note: These are illustrative scenarios for scoping, not pre-determined mandates from the organizers.)_

---

## Challenge Statement

> **Design a novel insurance service that delivers value _before_ users make critical life decisions, shifting from reactive compensation to proactive empowerment.**

Since full challenge specifications are still being rolled out, we can turn this concept into an actionable product direction.

## 1. What Are We Actually Building?

Imagine designing a new digital service or mobile app for If Insurance.

- **Scenario 1: Moving to a New Home**
  The user enters upcoming relocation details. The platform guides them through property checks, transit risks, and safety essentials for the new apartment, surfacing relevant coverage.
- **Scenario 2: Buying a Property**
  The platform demystifies hidden structural and liability risks, clarifying how different policies protect the investment _before_ signing the contract.
- **Scenario 3: First-Time Pet Ownership**
  The platform prepares users for vet bills, preventive care, and liability risks ahead of adoption, recommending tailored protection plans.

_Goal: Rather than stuffing every scenario into the prototype, our team should focus on delivering 1–2 killer user journeys._

---

## 2. Product Concept: "Life Moments"

_Not just an insurance purchase tool, but a proactive life decision companion._

### User Flow

1. **Select a Life Event**
   (e.g., Moving, Buying a Home, Starting a Job, Getting a Pet)
2. **Get a Personalized Risk & Action Checklist**
   The system highlights potential vulnerabilities and explains _why_ they matter at this exact stage.
3. **Receive Actionable Preventive Solutions & Coverage**
   Users see concrete preventive steps, existing policy gaps, and recommended modular coverage.
4. **Contextual, Timely Nudges**
   Automated reminders before moving day (e.g., transit checklists, key handover tips) rather than reaching out after a loss occurs.

### 💡 High-Conversion Growth Layer (to validate with the PO / mentors)

To boost **conversion rate, user retention, and engagement**, can we integrate third-party partner services and utilities?

- **Partnered Moving Ecosystem:** One-click booking with verified moving companies, alongside tool/trailer rentals (e.g., moving dollies, moisture meters, thermal leak detectors).
- **Relocation / Expat Onboarding Kit:** A tailored "Soft Landing in Finland" guide covering newcomers, families with kids, and pet owners with bundled multi-policy onboarding.
- **Value-Add Knowledge & Prevention Hub:** Exclusive post-purchase prevention guides (e.g., parquet floor maintenance, moisture prevention), directly incentivizing policy activation while reducing claim frequency.

## 4. Why Does If Insurance Care? (Business Value)

This is not just a feel-good helper tool; it directly drives core insurance KPIs:

- **Acquisition:** Lowers user hesitation by bundling tangible utilities (tools, vetted movers) with contextual insurance, improving funnel conversion.
- **Retention:** Evolves the brand touchpoint from an annual renewal email into an ongoing lifestyle advisor.
- **Risk Reduction (Loss Ratio):** Preventive kits, partner standards, and care guides prevent accidents before they turn into costly claims.

## 5. If's Existing Partner Ecosystem

### 5.1 Pet Care & Veterinary

- **24/7 Digital & Tele-veterinary Care**
  - **If Eläinapu** (with providers such as **Vireä Group / FirstVet**): round-the-clock free online vet consultations, video triage, and digital diagnoses for pet insurance policyholders.
- **Direct Billing Clinic Network (_Suorakorvaus_)**
  - Integrated with 90+ veterinary clinics and hospital chains across Finland (e.g., **Evidensia**, **Omaeläinsairaala**). Customers pay only their deductible (e.g., 35%) at the counter; If settles the rest directly with the clinic in real time.

### 5.2 Home Safety, Pest Control & Prevention

- **Moisture, Pest Control & Structural Prevention**
  - **Anticimex**: If's primary strategic partner for home loss prevention — on-site inspections and remediation for indoor moisture, wood-boring pests, and bedbugs (_lutikka_).
- **Smart Security & Monitoring**
  - Connected home alarm and sensor providers (such as **Verisure**): premium discounts and incentives for homes with linked smoke/leak sensors.

### 5.3 Automotive & Mobility

- **Certified Repair & Glass Replacement Network (_If Laatukorjaamot_)**
  - Nationwide accredited body shops and glass repair specialists; policyholders can bring damaged vehicles directly without pre-claim paperwork.
- **Roadside Assistance & Replacement Mobility**
  - Towing and roadside fleets (e.g., **Falck / Redgo**) and rental car agencies (e.g., **Hertz**, **Europcar**, **Avis**) supply instant replacement vehicles during repairs.

### 5.4 Healthcare & Occupational Wellbeing

- **Private Clinical Networks**
  - Collaborations with Finnish private healthcare providers such as **Mehiläinen** and **Docrates Cancer Center**: fast-track specialist appointments, remote physician access, and corporate health services.

### 5.5 Trade Unions & Professional Associations (_Ammattiliitot_)

- **70+ Finnish labor unions & professional bodies**, including **Insinööriliitto (IL)**, **TEK**, **Tradenomit**, **JHL**, and **PAM**.
  - Members get continuous 10%–15% insurance discounts and tailored group coverage through co-branded portals.

## The Challenge

This is not simply a "build another insurance app" challenge. The difficult part is identifying a real human need where an insurance company can provide meaningful value without making insurance itself the centre of the experience. The best idea might prevent something from going wrong, make a life change easier, help someone make a better decision, connect useful services together, or create something completely different. AI, data and automation can absolutely be part of the solution, but technology is not the goal. The goal is something people would genuinely value — with a believable reason why it would also make sense for If as a business.
