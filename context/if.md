# If Insurance: Home & Pet Services Research

Researched 2026-10-16. Focus: Finland (the challenge's home market). Sources are linked at the bottom. Prices and terms change, so verify before quoting numbers in the demo or pitch.

## TL;DR

- Home and pet are both real, sellable IF products in Finland with concrete service hooks.
- The strongest verified hooks are **moving features** on the home side (3-month cover in both homes, transport damage cover) and **free 24/7 tele-vet plus direct billing** on the pet side.
- **Recommendation:** build one combined journey, **"Moving with a pet"**. Lead with the moving context, since it is the brief's lead candidate and has the richest real product hooks. Pet is the second layer. Fallback: a standalone "Getting a pet" journey if the moving layer proves too thin.
- Several brief claims (Anticimex and Verisure partnerships, 90+ clinics, FirstVet visit counts) could not be confirmed on If's own pages. Treat them as placeholders until a mentor confirms them.

## 1. What IF offers (Finland)

### 1.1 Home insurance

**Products** ([If home insurance](https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/kotivakuutus))

| Product | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kerros- ja rivitalo (flat / terraced house) | Contents-only; monthly billing; online purchase |
| Omakotitalo (detached house) | Building + contents; "broadest" option; includes construction-phase cover |
| Paritalo (semi-detached) | Three coverage levels; condo-form online, real-estate-form via customer service |
| Mökkivakuutus (holiday home) | Covers vapaa-ajan asunto |
| Vuokranantajan / sijoitusasunto (landlord) | Legal protection plus rent-loss cover up to 12 months |
| Irtaimistovakuutus (contents) | Standalone contents cover |

**Coverage levels (flat / terraced):** perustaso (basic), laaja (extensive), laajaplus (extended plus).

**Limits:**
- Basic and extensive: up to €5,000 per item, €2,500 for a bicycle.
- Laajaplus: up to €17,000 per item, €5,000 for a bicycle.
- Valuables above these limits go through customer service.
- Deductibles (omavastuu) are not listed; they are shown only in the webshop.
- Premiums depend on coverage level, floor area and location. No prices are published.

**Moving-related features** (the most useful hooks for the demo):
- **Muuttoilmoitus / "Siirrä kotivakuutus":** the policy can be transferred when you move.
- **3-month overlap:** if contents are insured with If, cover applies in both the old and the new home for three months.
- **Transport damage:** the extensive level (laaja) covers items damaged during transport.

**Digital services:** webshop price calculator and comparison, Omat sivut (online account), If Mobiili app, e-invoices.

**Prevention content** ([Kodin turvallisuus](https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/kotivakuutus/kodin-turvallisuus)). Topics covered, all usable as checklist items:
- Water: water damage (vesivahinko), frozen pipes (putket jäässä), dishwasher installation (astianpesukoneen asennus).
- Weather: snow and thaw damage, flood preparedness, storm preparedness.
- Pests: bed bugs (luteet), household pests (kodin tuholaiset).
- Fire: smoke alarm check (palovaroitin), electrical fires, battery fires (e-scooter/e-bike), terrace fires (grill ashes), fireplace and candle safety.
- Security: burglary prevention (murtojen estäminen).
- Services: pest control service (tuholaistorjuntapalvelu, If's own, details not fetched); house maintenance plan for detached homes (omakotitalon kunnossapitosuunnitelma), where a construction professional visits the house.

**Not found on If's pages:** Anticimex, Verisure, moisture inspection, smart-home or alarm discounts. Alarm guidance is limited to smoke alarms.

### 1.2 Pet insurance

**Products** ([If pet insurance](https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/elainvakuutukset))
- Koiravakuutus (dog) and kissavakuutus (cat): customer chooses coverage and maximum compensation.
- Hevosvakuutus (horse): quote via customer service.

**Deductible:** 35% for dogs and cats, 25% for horses.

**Limits and prices:** no maximum amounts or premiums are published. Premiums depend on breed, age and municipality. Dog and cat quotes are available in the webshop.

**Free services:**
- **Etäeläinlääkäri (remote vet):** free, no deductible, 24/7 by chat or phone through Ifin eläinapu.
- **Suorakorvaus (direct billing):** at partner vet clinics, the clinic settles with If, so usually only the deductible is paid. The page says it works in "over 30 locations."
- **Fast claims:** up to 90% of pet claims processed within one day (per If's page).

**Other coverage:**
- Vet costs abroad on trips of up to one year in the Nordics, EU and Switzerland.
- Henkiturva: cover when a pet goes missing, dies or is stolen. No details given.

**Content:** "Vinkit koiran omistajalle" (tips for dog owners) and "Lemmikin kanssa" (tips for living with a pet).

### 1.3 Pet insurance, Estonia (benchmark only)

From [If Estonia](https://www.if.ee/en/eraklient/kindlustused/lemmiklooma-kindlustus), current from 1 Oct 2026. Not offered in Finland, but a useful model for how tiers can be structured in a prototype.

| Coverage | Lemmik | Superlemmik | Superlemmik+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medical treatment, per event | €600 | €1,600 | €3,000 |
| Dental emergency | — | €200 | €400 |
| Death (sum insured) | — | Included | Included |
| Theft or loss (sum insured) | — | Included | Included |
| Costs to find lost pet | — | €200 | €200 |
| Pet owner's liability | — | €2,000 | €5,000 |

- Starts from €10/month. Deductible is chosen by the customer.
- Operations, biopsies, MRI and CT are covered after a 3-month waiting period.
- New policies from 2 months to 7 years of age. Pets first insured before age 3 can renew medical cover without an age limit.

## 2. Home vs. pet for the hackathon

| Criterion | Home | Pet |
|---|---|---|
| Real, concrete product hooks | **Strong**: moving notice, 3-month dual-home cover, transport damage | **Strong**: free 24/7 tele-vet, direct billing |
| Prevention content to build a checklist from | **Strong**: detailed Kodin turvallisuus topic list | Medium: tips pages, not fetched in detail |
| Pricing data available | Weak: no prices, no deductibles published | Weak: no Finnish prices or limits published |
| Fit with brief's lead journey | **Strong**: "Moving" is the lead candidate | Medium: "Getting a pet" is scenario 3 |
| Demo "before value" story | Good: move-day checklist | **Strong**: "pet gets sick at 2am, here's what to do" |
| Business value for If | Retention through move, cross-sell of contents | Acquisition via first-time owners; higher claim-cost exposure |

**Caution:** the 2018 Finnish pet guide quotes a €2,000 treatment cap and €85,000 liability. These figures are old. Do not use them in the demo.

**Combined view:** the brief's growth layer already calls for a relocation kit that bundles multi-policy onboarding for "pet owners" ([context.md](context.md), section 2). Home plus pet fits this naturally.

## 3. Recommendation

### Option A (recommended): "Moving with a pet"
One journey where the user moves home with a dog or cat. Home gives the moving checklist and cover continuity. Pet gives vet access and liability before moving day.

**Why:**
- Uses the brief's lead candidate (moving) and its pet-owner bundle idea.
- Has the most verified, concrete If hooks to demo.
- Shows both "before" value (checklist) and "after" value (tele-vet, direct billing).

### Option B: "Getting a pet"
Simpler and pet-only. Weaker on home hooks and more reliant on unverified pricing.

### Option C: "Buying a home"
Richest content (structural risks, liability) but needs partner facts we could not verify, and heavier to build.

## 4. Proposed risk engine (mock data, sourced rules)

**Inputs:**
- Move date, origin and destination home
- Home type: flat, terraced, detached, rental, holiday home
- Floor area and rough movable-property value
- Coverage level (perustaso / laaja / laajaplus)
- Pets: species, age, currently insured?
- Municipality (affects premium, per If)

**Outputs (checklist items, each tagged with its source):**

*Home*
- Notify insurer and transfer policy (If: muuttoilmoitus / Siirrä kotivakuutus).
- Keep the old policy active for the 3-month overlap if contents are insured with If (If home page).
- Check transport damage cover; laaja needed (If home page).
- Test smoke alarms before the move (If: palovaroitin).
- New home: check frozen pipes, dishwasher install, pest and bed bug signs (If: prevention topics).
- Detached house: maintenance plan visit (If: omakotitalon kunnossapitosuunnitelma).

*Pet*
- Activate remote vet (Etäeläinlääkäri) and save the number (If pet page).
- Find a direct-billing clinic near the new home (If: Suorakorvaus, mock map for demo).
- If moving abroad: vet cover on trips up to one year in Nordics/EU/Switzerland (If pet page); confirm for longer stays.
- Check pet owner's liability limit (Finnish figure to confirm; Estonian benchmark €2k–5k).
- Pet-proofing the new home (generic advice, not an If source; label as ours).

**Action layer (partner services, mock):** movers, tool rental, pest inspection (Anticimex as placeholder only), pet transport. Label partners as placeholders until confirmed.

**Demo story sketch:**
Before: user moves with a cat, the old policy is cancelled on move day, and there is no pet cover in the new area. After: the checklist keeps the 3-month overlap, activates tele-vet on day one, and shows the nearest direct-billing clinic.
