# If Insurance (Finland) consumer products and how they connect, with a focus on moving home

Research date: 2026-10-17. Primary market: Finland (if.fi). Swedish notes come from if.se. Estonia and Norway/Denmark were not researched in depth.

## 0. How to read this report

Each claim is tagged with its evidence level:

- **[Src]** Stated on an If page I fetched (if.fi unless noted). Fetching was done through a summarising tool, so wording and numbers are paraphrased, not verified verbatim. Re-check the key numbers before using them in a pitch.
- **[Search]** Taken from a search-result snippet or a third-party article, not from an If page.
- **[Inf]** My inference. Not stated by If.

Main limitation: the home-insurance terms PDF (Kodin omaisuusvakuutusehdot, Ehto OMA 200.27, https://www.if.fi/globalassets/fi/pdf/ehdot/kotivakuutusehdot.pdf) could not be read. It is AES-256 encrypted and my PDF renderer is not installed. As a result, I could **not verify** the clause-level rules on storage, goods in transit by movers, vacant property, or damage caused by movers. These are listed as open questions in section 4.

Terminology: If Finland does not use "Basic / Medium / Extended". Its home tiers are **Perus** (basic), **Laaja** (comprehensive) and **LaajaPlus** (extended) [Src].

**Home insurance tiers (kotivakuutus, flat / detached / cottage) [Src]:**

- **Perus:** contents, liability, legal protection, plus fire, leak, electrical and natural-hazard damage. Items up to 5,000 EUR, bicycles up to 5,000 EUR.
- **Laaja:** everything in Perus, plus sudden breakage (for example a cracked phone or a broken washing machine), theft and vandalism, and fixed fittings (up to 10,000 EUR). Same 5,000 EUR per-item cap.
- **LaajaPlus:** higher limits (items up to 17,000 EUR, bicycles up to 5,000 EUR, art and jewellery up to 50,000 EUR in total), smaller age deductions, lower travel-baggage deductible (50 EUR), moisture and pest damage to the structure up to 15,000 EUR per claim (detached/cottage), and a switching promise.
- Contents deductible: 150, 250 or 450 EUR. The most common choice is 250 EUR [Src].
- Full-value insurance (täysarvo). The value of the property is assessed after a loss, not when you buy [Src].
- Liability (vastuuvakuutus) up to 200,000 EUR and legal protection (oikeusturva) up to 10,000 EUR, both included with contents cover [Src].
- Temporary housing: if the home is uninhabitable after an insured event, 85% of extra living costs are covered for up to 12 months [Src].
- Depreciation: appliances and bicycles 9% per year, glasses 15%, phones 25%. Water-damage payouts depend on pipe age (100% for 0–29 years, 75% for 30–50, 50% over 50) [Src, detached-house page].

**Detached house specifics [Src]:** covers the building at full value, plus the yard. Yard items (play house, hot tub, car shelter, barrel sauna) up to 5,000 EUR each. Free maintenance plan (Anticimex visit every four years, written report, phone advice) in Laaja and LaajaPlus. Free pest-control advice, with bait by post or on-site treatment, in Laaja and LaajaPlus.

**Cottage specifics [Src]:** Laaja and LaajaPlus cover damage from wild animals and breakage of contents. Boats (rowboat, canoe, or dinghy up to 3,500 EUR). Vacancy rules are not stated on the page.

**Landlord specifics [Src]:** two levels (narrower and broader). Rent-loss cover pays up to 12 months, with a deductible of 50% of one month's rent. Tenant-deliberate-damage add-on has conditions (police report, policy running at least 6 months before the damage). Short-term rentals such as Airbnb are only partly covered [Src; the rental-related conditions are on the Laaja/Kotivakuutus pages].

**Notes on "Kodin turvallisuus":** the page is safety advice (fire, water, burglary, storm prevention) linked to home insurance. It does not describe an alarm product, Verisure partnership, or discount [Src]. I found no If page confirming a Verisure partnership in Finland [Inf/unverified].

### 1.2 Moving-related home rules [Src unless noted]

- Home contents cover stays valid in both the old and the new home for **three months** (kotivakuutus page). Laaja covers breakage of belongings during the move (kerrostalo-rivitalo page). Confirmed on if.fi.
- You can move the policy to a new address in Omat sivut (kerrostalo page) and via the "Siirrä kotivakuutus" moving form (link on home pages; not fetched directly).
- A Finnish Oikotie article co-sponsored by If (search snippet) says the policy does not transfer automatically, and the address change has to be made with your own insurer [Search].

### 1.3 Car insurance (autovakuutus)

Source: https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/autovakuutus [Src]

- **Liikennevakuutus** (traffic/third-party liability): mandatory. Covers the other party's injuries and property. Does not cover your own car's damage.
- **Osakasko** (partial casco): adds theft, vandalism, fire, glass, towing, legal protection, personal cover, and animal collisions.
- **Täyskasko** (full casco): adds collision cover (törmäysturva), parking cover (pysäköintiturva), buy-out (lunastusturva) and a replacement car (up to 40 days).
- Theft is covered under osakasko and täyskasko, not liikennevakuutus. Deductible: 200 EUR.
- Collision deductible for täyskasko: recommended 500 EUR. Other casco deductibles: typically 200 EUR. Glass: 200 EUR at partner garages, 300 EUR elsewhere.
- Brand and financing casco variants exist (see the benefit programme page).
- Price-related published examples: minimum premium 25 EUR; average windscreen repair ~70 EUR, replacement ~750 EUR. Liikennevakuutus bonus typically 30–80%, building to full in about five years without claims. Full prices are only available via the online calculator [Src].
- Moving and car: the page says the premium depends on the home address but does not say how a move or postcode change is handled [Src/gap]. Electric cars and motorcycles, moped, van, and trailer are separate pages.

### 1.4 Travel insurance (matkavakuutus)

Source: https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/matkavakuutus [Src]

- **Jatkuva (continuous):** covers the whole year for trips up to 45 days each. Suits frequent travellers. Covers domestic trips too (domestic medical is an add-on, estimated under 20 EUR per year for a 30-year-old).
- **Määräaikainen (fixed-term):** one trip, foreign travel only, price depends on destination and length. No age limit. Online purchase for under-27s or home-insurance holders.
- Medical costs abroad: no deductible, no upper limit. Trip cancellation or interruption due to illness: up to 5,000 EUR per person per trip. Delay cover for transport (including connecting flights). Ambulance flight to Finland. 24/7 assistance line (FI/EN/SV).
- Baggage add-on (matkatavaravakuutus): covers theft and damage if your home insurance does not. Note that the **travel baggage of the home insurance** already covers the same thing, so the page recommends checking home insurance first [Src].
- Cancellation cover requires the policy to be bought at least 3 days before departure [Src].
- Sport: extreme sports like tandem flying are covered, regular practice needs urheiluvakuutus [Src].

### 1.5 Accident insurance (tapaturmavakuutus)

Source: https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/henkilovakuutukset/tapaturmavakuutus [Src]

- Covers accidents at home, in traffic and in leisure (24/7).
- Basic tier: medical costs, permanent disability lump sum up to 80,000 EUR, accidental death 20,000 EUR. Physiotherapy only after surgery or casting.
- Laaja tier: adds sports-related strains and some age-related degeneration; physiotherapy up to 5 sessions per injury, 10 per year. Age at purchase 15–74, valid to 80. Basic: 15–79 at purchase, valid to 100.
- Medical-cost cap of 50,000 EUR for online purchases. Illness is not covered. Competitive or high-risk sport needs urheiluvakuutus.
- Price: no figures; depends on age, residence, tier and deductible. Default deductible 0 EUR.

### 1.6 Health and life products (personal insurance)

- **Vakavan sairauden turva (serious illness):** tax-free lump sum of 20,000–50,000 EUR for ten listed conditions (e.g. cancer, heart attack, stroke, MS). Ages 15–59 to apply, cover to 65. Cancer claims have a 90-day waiting period. Price example: about 17 EUR per month for a 35-year-old with 50,000 EUR payout [Src]. https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/henkilovakuutukset/vakavan-sairauden-turva
- **Sairausvakuutus (illness insurance)**: listed, only a link on the serious illness page. Coverage not verified [Src/gap].
- **Henkivakuutus (life), Terveysturva (health security), Toimeentulovakuutus (income protection), Vauvavakuutus, Lapsivakuutus, Urheiluvakuutus:** listed on the product hub [Src]. Their terms were not fetched.
- Personal insurance is part of the benefit programme, but the programme says Primus accident and life policies are not eligible (see 1.9) [Src].

### 1.7 Pets

Source hub: https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/elainvakuutukset [Src]

- **Koiravakuutus (dog):** eligible dogs 6 weeks to 7 years. Vet care for accidents and illness, imaging, rehabilitation up to 300 EUR per year, death/loss/theft value (until the year the dog turns 10), liability up to 85,000 EUR per claim. Annual maximum compensation tiers: 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 or 4,000 EUR. Vet-cost deductible: a fixed 35%. Liability deductible 150 EUR. 14-day illness waiting period, 48-hour accident waiting period [Src].
- **Kissavakuutus (cat):** same structure. Eligible cats 6 weeks to 7 years. Annual limit 1,000–4,000 EUR. Deductible 35% [Src].
- **Hevosvakuutus (horse)**: listed only.
- Price examples are claim examples, not premiums: two vet visits (150 and 250 EUR) plus 50 EUR medicine paid at 65% = 292.50 EUR (dog and cat pages). Imaging costs for cats: X-rays ~250 EUR, ultrasound ~400 EUR, CT ~1,000 EUR [Src].
- Benefits outside the contract: suorakorvaus (direct billing at partner clinics, over 90 clinics), Etäeläinlääkäri / If Eläinapu (tele-vet, 24/7, run with partner Vireä Group) [Src].

### 1.8 Valuables and specific items

- No standalone valuables product found on the product hub. Valuables are inside the home contents policy under the per-item limit (5,000 EUR in Perus/Laaja; 17,000 EUR in LaajaPlus) with a cap of 50,000 EUR for art and jewellery in LaajaPlus [Src].
- Bicycles are a separate cap: 5,000 EUR on all tiers in the detached-house page; 2,500 EUR in some older summaries [Search, not used].
- Example of depreciation: a 2021 bike stolen in 2025 is paid at 91% of a new equivalent under LaajaPlus [Src, kerrostalo page].

### 1.9 Loyalty and bundle programme: Ifin etuohjelma

Source: https://www.if.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/asiakasedut/if-etuohjelma [Src]

- **Taso 1 (tier 1): 10%** when you hold one qualifying policy and receive insurance documents electronically in Omat sivut.
- **Taso 2 (tier 2): up to 15%** when you hold at least two qualifying policies from home, casco and personal. Qualifying pairs include casco + home, home + personal, casco + personal.
- Qualifying: home (owned or rented), landlord, cottage, dog, cat, horse, forest, continuous baggage, passenger car, van, brand casco, financing casco (listed inconsistently), motorcycle, moped, snowmobile, boat, trailer, ATV, health care costs, serious illness, disability, life.
- Not eligible: liability traffic insurance (liikennevakuutus), Primus accident and life, Joustoturva life, hospital care protection, ainaispalo/-metsä, fixed-term travel insurance, and Toyota/Lexus brand casco started before 20.4.2024.
- Mechanics: applied at the start of the new insurance year. Once you have two qualifying policies, 15% applies to all other eligible policies. Discounts do not go down for existing customers at renewal.
- **"If Plus" / bonus tiers:** I could not find these names on If's Finnish pages. The only tiers are Taso 1 and Taso 2 [Src]. The Swedish page mentions a welcome discount instead (10% + 10% in year one, not applicable to Finland) [Src, if.se].
- Car page also shows the same 10% (car-only) / 15% (with home) figures [Src].
- Detached-house page says bundling gives 10% [Src]. These two statements are not fully consistent with the Taso 2 rule (15% with two qualifying policies), so use the programme page as the reference.
- Whether dog/cat insurance qualifies: the programme page lists dog, cat and horse as qualifying. The dog page says up to 15% but does not confirm it. Treat as qualifying, but verify [Src/conflict].

### 1.11 Legal protection and liability

- Legal protection (oikeusturva) is included with home contents up to 10,000 EUR and with the cottage up to 10,000 EUR; legal-aid deductible 20% (minimum 170 EUR) [Src].
- Liability is included with home insurance up to 200,000 EUR [Src].
- Car legal protection is part of osakasko and täyskasko (20% deductible, minimum 200 EUR) [Src].
- A standalone legal-protection product is listed for business customers only, not private [Src hub].

### 1.12 Sweden (if.se) differences

Source: https://www.if.se/privat/forsakringar/hemforsakring [Src]

- Tiers are Grundförsäkring, Hemförsäkring Extra and Stor hemförsäkring, not Perus/Laaja/LaajaPlus.
- Temporary housing and storage: up to two years if the home becomes uninhabitable.
- Travel: 45 days of travel cover built into home insurance (medical, theft abroad).
- Liability 5 MSEK, legal costs up to 300,000 SEK. Bicycles up to 35,000 SEK.
- Moving: you must update the policy. A single flow sets up the new policy and cancels the old one.
- Welcome discount: 10% first year + 10% for digital payment.
- Sickness and accident insurance are separate in Sweden (not included in home).

## 2. Product map: how the products connect

### 2.1 Overlaps (same event, two products)

1. **Travel baggage**: home contents (all tiers) covers luggage on a trip; the travel baggage add-on overlaps. If's own guidance is to check home first [Src].
2. **Liability**: included in home (200,000 EUR), cottage, dog/cat (85,000 EUR for dog), and car (liikennevakuutus for third parties). Multiple liability policies can respond to one claim [Inf].
3. **Legal protection**: home (10,000 EUR), car (osakasko/täyskasko), cottage. Multiple sources can cover the same dispute [Inf, consistent with If pages].
4. **Theft**: home contents covers theft from the home, car (osakasko) covers theft of the car, and travel covers theft abroad under baggage add-on. A stolen item from a car is generally a car-policy/contents question, and the page does not say which applies [Inf/gap].
5. **Accident**: accident insurance and home contents do not overlap on personal injury. Accident insurance covers injury at home, in traffic and in leisure [Src].

### 2.2 Dependencies (one product needed for another)

- Home contents must be in place before a bundle discount is applied (the discount is for two qualifying policies) [Src].
- Detached-house maintenance plan and pest control are only in Laaja/LaajaPlus [Src].
- Landlord rent-loss and tenant-damage add-ons require the landlord policy to run before the damage [Src].
- Car "Lasiturva" and "Pysäköintiturva" only exist in osakasko/täyskasko, not in liikennevakuutus [Src].

### 2.3 Complements

- Home contents + travel (fixed-term or continuous): trip cancellation and medical cover sit with travel; belongings at home sit with home [Src].
- Home contents + serious illness + accident: personal layer.
- Car (osakasko/täyskasko) + home: the bundle discount applies only when two qualifying policies are held [Src].
- Dog or cat + home: bundle discount [Src].

## 3. Moving journey (Finland)

Stages are my structure. Facts marked [Src] are from the pages above. Risks and actions are [Inf] unless noted.

### Stage 1: Planning (2–3 months before)

- **Home contents**: keep the current policy. Plan the transfer. Moves are covered in both homes for 3 months [Src].
- **Gap**: a policy that does not transfer automatically (Oikotie/If snippet) [Search]. Customer action: update the address in Omat sivut or with the moving form before the move.
- **Car**: if the new address changes the postcode, premium changes. Ask If. No documented process [Gap].
- **Legal protection**: if a partner moves out of the household, their legal-protection cover ends (Verkkouutiset via search) [Search].

### Stage 2: About 4 weeks before

- **Home contents**: choose the tier for the new home. A flat may stay at Laaja; a detached house or cottage needs its own product [Inf].
- **Cross-sell**: move from apartment to detached house is a new product (omakotitalo) with yard and building cover [Src].
- **Travel**: if moving abroad, check fixed-term vs continuous travel; cancellation cover requires purchase 3 days before departure [Src].
- **Pets**: a new home may require a new pet policy or an updated contract [Inf].

### Stage 3: Packing

- **Home contents, Laaja**: covers breakage during the move [Src].
- **Home contents, Perus**: does not include sudden breakage, so fragile items (phone, TV) are not covered during packing [Inf from tier definitions].
- **Valuables**: check that items (art, jewellery) are within per-item caps (5,000 EUR in Laaja; 17,000 EUR in LaajaPlus; 50,000 EUR total for art and jewellery in LaajaPlus) [Src].
- **Gap**: items in storage, goods carried by a moving company, and damage caused by movers are not confirmed in If's terms [gap, PDF not read].

### Stage 4: Moving day

- **Home contents**: both addresses covered for 3 months [Src]. Confirm the new address is in the policy so that a claim is not disputed.
- **Car**: the car is used to transport items. Osakasko/täyskasko covers theft; car damage from loading is not confirmed [gap].
- **Accident**: personal injury while moving (lifting) is covered under accident insurance (home, traffic, leisure) [Src].
- **Gap**: a damaged item in a rental van or transported by movers. Check whether the mover's liability applies first [Inf].

### Stage 5: First weeks at the new home

- **Home contents**: confirm the policy has moved. Tenant or owner status, building type and floor area drive the price. Full-value insurance is valued after the claim, so record the new home's contents [Src].
- **Old home**: the old address stops being covered after 3 months [Src].
- **Discounts**: the benefit programme applies at the start of the new insurance year [Src].
- **Temporary housing**: if the new home becomes uninhabitable after an insured event, 85% of extra costs up to 12 months are covered [Src].
- **Landlord**: if you rent out the old home, switch to landlord insurance. Short-term rental (e.g. Airbnb) needs advance notice to If [Src].
- **Car**: a longer commute changes mileage and premium. Notify If [Inf].
- **Pets**: pet insurance follows the animal, not the address. Confirm the policy is not location-restricted [gap].

## 4. Coverage gaps and cross-sell opportunities at moving

1. **Goods in transit with a mover, storage units, movers' damage, vacant property**: these would sit in the home contents terms (PDF not readable). **Open question.** Treat as unverified.
2. **Car insurance and address change**: the process is not published on the car page [Src/gap].
3. **Pet policy and address change**: not published [gap].
